## Limits

- Attestations and the evidence hashes and references are operator assertions.
  The service verifies identity, termination, ownership, witnesses and replay; it
  cannot verify independent review, and the CLI cannot make an assertion true.
- `recovery-options` discloses the held generation's process identity to both
  credential scopes; the classification is tracked in
  [#926](https://github.com/chrisbanes/ensemble/issues/926).
- Eligibility is advisory; the service stays authoritative.
- `content-redacted` also covers an exact value longer than a command accepts, and
  `omittedCount`/`evidenceAvailable` count the whole task's generations, so a gap can
  overstate incompleteness; neither can report a complete view falsely.
- Two concurrent first submissions of one document can choose different keys; the
  service applies one and refuses the other.
- No live runtime, real process termination, second machine or private transport.
