## Cleanup

- **Ownership before bootout:** `launchctl print` showed the exact Node executable, the
  built CLI, the data directory, the plist and the port; `ps -p <pid> -o command=`
  matched `node cli.js operator <data> <port>` exactly.
- **Bootout:** `launchctl bootout` exited 0. The PID was gone about 0.5 s later and the
  loopback port was closed.
- **Disclosed race:** the driver's `launchctl print` immediately after bootout did not
  yet report the label absent, because launchd tears the job down asynchronously. The
  driver therefore recorded a hold ("cleanup not proved") although every functional step
  had passed. A controller recheck at about 13:47Z, shortly after the run ended at
  13:46:13Z, showed `launchctl print` exit 113, the label not listed (`launchctl list`
  count 0) and `ps -p` for the recorded PID exit 1; the driver had already recorded the
  port closed. Only then was the scratch root removed.
  The non-secret outputs (the three readiness reads and the task list) were retained.
