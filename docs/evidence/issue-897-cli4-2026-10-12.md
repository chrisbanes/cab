Reverse paths: selections add/remove, links link/unlink, credential reference
set/clear (all revision-guarded); `place-task` is repeatable until the task has an
assignment; `refresh` is repeatable. Intentionally irreversible, flagged in help,
output and docs: removing a selection discards its definition, activation has no
deactivate command, a comment decision is final (deny is the counterpart of
approve), and a posted comment cannot be edited or retracted.

## Limits

- `operation get` does not read `github.place`, `delivery.refresh` or comment
  receipts; they are settled by rerunning with the same key. A rerun after a lost
  placement reports the placement from the task read, without its receipt.
- `send-comment --review-id R` replays the review with key `R` to read its
  decision; an `R` that is not an existing review creates a pending review that is
  then refused, and nothing is posted.
- The service can refuse a comment send's receipt with 409 after recording the
  operation (its existing visibility fence). The CLI then finds the operation in
  the task read and reports an unknown outcome (exit 6), so a rerun replays rather
  than reposts. A `--request-id` that names another operation with other material,
  unknown to this journal, is reported the same way; nothing is posted in either
  case.
- Comment rejections are classified by message text until typed errors land
  (#873).
- The `unavailable` observation state (`redaction-unavailable`) is mapped but not
  exercised through the CLI.
- No live GitHub, runtime, second machine or private transport.
