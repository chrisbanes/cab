## Findings

- Adopting current instructions on a completed lead blocks its follow-up.
  `assignment.apply` raises the assignment version, and a follow-up resumes only a
  result recorded at the current version, so `assignment apply-current` followed by
  `task follow-up` exits 4 and the lead cannot be resumed
  (`test/cli8-journey-harness.test.ts` reproduces it). The design promises adoption
  for the next turn, so this is a defect in promised behaviour. The journey sends the
  follow-up under Stop, adopts, then resumes; Stop is task-wide and requests
  cancellation of the task's other active work. Tracked in
  [#935](https://github.com/chrisbanes/ensemble/issues/935), not repaired here:
  changing either rule changes an accepted contract.
- With `HOME` pointing at a directory without a login keychain, Keychain storage
  through `auth login` waits indefinitely on `security` instead of failing as
  `credential-store-unavailable`. Ordinary sessions are unaffected.
