## Limits

- `operation get` does not read CLI5 types; an uncertain outcome is settled by
  retrying with the same key and checked with `task get`, `question get` or
  `approval get` (CLI8 and #897 may revisit).
- Native question answering through the CLI is proved against a fake service
  asserting the exact `question.native.answer` body; the real service path is
  covered by the existing native question service tests, not by a CLI journey.
- No live runtime, second machine or private transport. Stop is best-effort and
  observed with the fixture runtime only.
- The inbox still scans every task per read (#875).
- Unresolved journal keys persist until retried or superseded by `--request-id`.
- Post-commit Stop work (native-question cancellation and the bounded observation)
  and Resume's scheduler wake run once, after the receipt commits. If they fail, the
  command reports an unknown outcome and a retry replays the receipt without
  repeating them; the Stop hold and stop targets are already durable, as with the
  keyless Stop.
- An approval whose target is withheld cannot be decided through the CLI: the
  service compares the target exactly and fails closed (409).
